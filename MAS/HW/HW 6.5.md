Our basis is not orthogonal so we cannot use the QR factorization. Instead we use:

$$
A^Tb=A^TAx
$$

$$
A^T = \begin{bmatrix}
2 & -2 & 2 \\
1 & 0 & 3
\end{bmatrix}
$$
$$
A^TA = b
$$
