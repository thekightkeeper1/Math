# Orthogonal basis
To calculate $\left[\mathbf{y}\right]_{\mathcal{W}}$ given $\vec{y}$ and an orthogonal set $U$ as a basis to $W$:
$$
c_{j} = \frac{y \cdot u_{j}}{\left| \left| u_{j} \right| \right| ^2}
$$
