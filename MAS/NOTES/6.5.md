# Least squares 
If we need an answer, even when one does not exist, we minmize the distance of $b-A\vec{x}$. So we need to find the single $\hat{x}$ such that for all $\vec{x}$ in $A$:
$$
b-A\hat{x} \leq b-A\vec{x}
$$
In particular, we just project $\vec{b}$ onto $\text{Col } A$

We know that the difference between the projection and the actual vector is orthogonal to the columns of $A$
![[meta/images/Pasted image 20250407113659.png]]
$$
a_{j} -\cdot (b - A\hat{x}) = 0
$$

which looks smth like:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{j_{1}} \\
a_{j_{2}} \\
a_{j_{3}}
\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}
x_{1} \\
x_{2} \\
x_{3}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
