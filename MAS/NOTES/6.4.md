Gram-Schmidt Process lets us factor a LI matrix $A$ 
$$
A = QR
$$
Where $Q$ is an orthogonal matrix and $R$ is a triangular matrix

First, use GS process to get Q, then note that for orthogonal matrices:
$$
Q^TQ = I
$$
So instead of calculating the inverse of this matrix, to solve for R, we can just:
$$
Q^TA=R
$$
