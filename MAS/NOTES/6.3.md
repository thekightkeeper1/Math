# Properties of orthogonal projections:
$$
\text{if } y \in W=\text{Span}\left\{
\vec{u}_1, \dots , \vec{u_n}
\right\}
\text{  then proj}_{w}\space y = y
$$

The vector $\hat{y}$ is **the best approximation to y by elements of W**
See the image of an orthogonal projection above to get an idea of why.
