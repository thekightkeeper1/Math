- orthogonal if $\langle \vec{v}, \vec{u}\rangle=0$
# Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality
$$
\left| \langle u,v \rangle \right| \leq ||u||\space ||v|| 
$$

# Triangle Inequality
![[meta/images/Pasted image 20250416095320.png]]
$$
\left| \left| \vec{u} + \vec{v} \right|  \right| \leq ||\vec{u}||+ ||\vec{v}|| 
$$
