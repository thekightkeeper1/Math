# Orthogonal
Two lines are perpendicular if and only if:
$$
\left| \left| u-v \right| \right| = \left| \left| u-(-v) \right| \right| 
$$

# Length of a vector
The notation is
$$
\left| \left| v \right| \right| =\sqrt{ v \cdot v }
$$ 

# Normalization
This means to get the vector whose length is 1, and whose direction is the same as the original.
just do:
$$
\frac{v}{\left| \left| v \right| \right| }
$$

You can see how every vector is just $c$ times its base vector. So if we divide by that $c$, then we get it.

# Distance between vectors
Intuitively, the distance between 2 scalars is their difference, so do the same for vectors:
$$
\left| \left| u - v \right| \right| 
$$
