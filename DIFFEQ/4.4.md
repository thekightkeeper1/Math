# Method of undetermined coefficients
## Equations
### Non-homogenous ODE has a RHS called the forcing function.
$$
ay'' + by' + cy = f(t) 
$$

### A nice function is below. This is required for using undetermined coeffs.
$$
\begin{align}
& f(t) = Ct^{m}e^{rt} \\
& \text{or} \\
& f(t) = Ct^{m}e^{\alpha t}(\sin\beta t + \cos\beta t)
\end{align}
$$
### The solution we guess is either of these. if $f(t)$ has trig then so does our guess.
Our guess is also known as the particular solution, since it doesn't represent every possible function that satisfies the ODE.
$$
y_p =t^{s}(A_mt^{m}+\dots+A_1t+A+0)e^{\alpha t}
$$
$$
y_p =t^{s}(A_mt^{m}+\dots+A_1t+A)e^{\alpha t}\cos\beta t + t^{s}(B_mt^{m}+\dots+B_1t+B)e^{\alpha t}\sin\beta t
$$
### The general solution:
This does represent every $y$ which satisfies our ODE.
Note that $y_h$ is 
$$
y = \underbrace{ y_h }_{ \text{homogenous} } + \underbrace{ y_p }_{ \text{particular} } 
$$
