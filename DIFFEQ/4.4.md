# Method of undetermined coefficients
## Equations
### (1) Non-homogenous ODE has a RHS called the forcing function.
$$
ay'' + by' + cy = f(t) 
$$

### (2) A nice function is below. This is required for using undetermined coeffs.
$$
\begin{align}
& f(t) = Ct^{m}e^{rt} \\
& \text{or} \\
& f(t) = Ct^{m}e^{\alpha t}(\sin\beta t + \cos\beta t)
\end{align}
$$
### (3) The particular solution.
- The solution obtained from non-homogenous analysis
- It is also the guess that we plug into the ODE
- If LHS has sin or cos the  $y_p$ 

$$
y_p =t^{s}(A_mt^{m}+\dots+A_1t+A+0)e^{\alpha t}
$$
$$
y_p =t^{s}(A_mt^{m}+\dots+A_1t+A)e^{\alpha t}\cos\beta t + t^{s}(B_mt^{m}+\dots+B_1t+B)e^{\alpha t}\sin\beta t
$$
### (4) The general solution:
- It is the sum of the  solution obtained from the homogenous equation, plus that from our non-homogenous solution, aka the particular.
- This does represent every $y$ which satisfies our ODE.
- $y_h$ is $0$ when we plug into our ode, so thats why we solve $y_p$ and $y_h$ separately, then combine.
$$
y = \underbrace{ y_h }_{ \text{homogenous} } + \underbrace{ y_p }_{ \text{particular} } 
$$

## Methods:  
We need to get our guess for the [[DIFFEQ/4.4#The solution we guess is either of these. if $f(t)$ has trig then so does our guess.|particular-solution]] (3)
1. **Solve the homogenous** if it wants general solution. Else just get the roots $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$.
2. Check how many times you see the roots in $f(t)$. The variable$s$ is equal to that number.
3. The variable $m$ is equal to highest degree in $f(t)$ 
4. The variables $r$ and/or $\alpha$ are also given by $f(t)$ 

-  If you have a sum of forcing functions, then use superposition
- The if you have even one trig function, then you *must* assume that $y(p)$ is both $\sin$ and $\cos$
