# Problem 2
$$
\begin{align}
z=4x^{2}+y^2 \\
\end{align}
$$
So for this one, you just evaluate what the x-y plane looks like for different values of z:
$$
\begin{align}
z=0  \\
\implies 4x^{2}=y^{2} \\
\implies x=y=0
\end{align}
$$
Thus we have a point $(0,0,0)$
$$
\begin{align}
z=1: \\
1=x^{2}+4y^{2}
\end{align}
$$
That would give us an elipse.
$$
\begin{align}
z=2: \\
2=x^2+4y^{2} \\
\implies_{1}=\frac{x}{2}
\end{align}
$$
